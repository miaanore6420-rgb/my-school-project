Echoes of a Forgotten Event

A memory does not disappear. It changes rooms.

It was not the house that broke.
It was the night inside us.

At 3:17, the call came.
At 3:18, you were gone.
At 3:19, I learned how quickly a family
can turn a tragedy into a locked room.

Years later, I still hear the door.
Years later, I still reach for the handle.

And every time the clock forgets to move,
your red coat crosses the hallway—
carrying the weather of that night
back into me.
